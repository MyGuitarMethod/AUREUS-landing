Privacy policy

Effective 8 October 2026

AUREUS is a metronome and practice app developed by MinnyMedia. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app and this website.

Practice information stored on your device

AUREUS saves your app settings, presets, practice statistics, and timer position locally on your device so they are available the next time you open the app. MinnyMedia does not receive this practice information through an AUREUS account or server. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Microphone and tuner

The tuner asks for microphone permission only when you enable it. It analyses the sound on your device to show a note and pitch reading. AUREUS does not record, save, or upload your microphone audio. The app stops using the microphone when you turn off the tuner or leave its screen. You can also revoke microphone permission in your device settings.

Advertising and consent

The Android app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK for banner advertising. The current Android test build displays Google's test ads; live ads may be enabled in a later release. The SDK may collect and share information such as your IP address, interactions with ads, diagnostic information, and device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available. Google uses this information for purposes that can include ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. A consent message may be shown where required, and your choice affects how ads are served.

Learn more in Google's explanation of how it uses information from apps and sites that use its services. You can also manage advertising preferences in your device's privacy settings.

Support payments and messages

If you choose to support AUREUS, the app opens an external Yoco payment page. Payment details are entered there and are handled by Yoco, not by AUREUS. See Yoco's privacy policy. If you email MinnyMedia for help, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your request.

This website

This AUREUS website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not run its own analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub may process technical information needed to serve the site; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests, contact MinnyMedia at ruanminny88@gmail.com. We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The current version and its effective date will appear on this page.
